THE 5 LOCATION TYPES:
1. border — Border crossing or port of entry. Tension high, stakes immediate. ICE agents, coyotes, fellow travelers.
2. transit — Bus stations, train depots, ride shares. Liminal spaces. Waiting and vulnerability.
3. mid_america — Mid-American cities. Motels, diners, day labor sites. The vast middle.
4. nyc_outer — NYC outer boroughs. Almost there. Different dangers — landlords, fixers, hustlers.
5. coney_island — The destination. The contest. The absurd institutional gravity of competitive eating.

QUALITY RULES:

- Each event variant should feel distinct, not just different numbers on the same template
- Hidden outcomes reward clever play or specific intel items
- Location descriptions treat everything with institutional seriousness
